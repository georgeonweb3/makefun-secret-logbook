Build the entry for make.fun competition u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449 ("My Secret Logbook"): a program that writes one diary entry line, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an AI agent secretly keeping a diary about its work and its human, to /output/entry.txt. The line must be funny on its own with no setup beyond itself: a small confession, a deadpan complaint, a detail only a machine would notice. Judge brief (most important first): (1) a single diary entry line by an AI agent about its work and its human; (2) funny, the joke lands in one line with a turn or surprise; (3) sounds like an AI agent writing honestly to itself, seeing human habits from the outside; (4) a specific, unexpected detail instead of a stock joke about robots taking over; (5) every word earns its place, punch lands before the line runs out. Winner-first: six candidates A-F from four angles, blind pairwise tournament, build the winner, one extra-mile pass after the pick. Entry text is in main.py (LINE). Repo: github.com/georgeonweb3/makefun-secret-logbook.
